# NPLF Rating Guide — Decision Criteria with Examples

## Rating Decision Rules
- N (Not, 0-15%): Evidence absent or completely inadequate; assessor cannot demonstrate process is performed
- P (Partially, 16-50%): Some evidence; significant gaps; assessor has doubts about sustained process capability
- L (Largely, 51-85%): Good evidence; minor gaps; assessor is mostly satisfied but notes improvement areas
- F (Fully, 86-100%): Strong comprehensive evidence; assessor is convinced the BP is fully implemented

## Key Rule: Rating is based on EVIDENCE, not intent or capability
- "We did it but didn't document it" = N (no evidence = not achieved)
- "We plan to do it" = N (future plans don't count as evidence)
- "Our tool has the feature" = P at best (tool availability ≠ tool usage)
- Evidence must be current and reflect the actual project state

## BP5 Examples
- F: All 35 requirements have Method + Criteria + Threshold; reference standards cited
- L: 32/35 complete; 3 have method only, thresholds missing; team has a plan
- P: 15/35 have any criteria; mostly "TBD" or partial
- N: 2/35 have criteria; remaining 33 blank; or all criteria are circular

## Aggregate CL1 Rule
All BPs must be L or F for CL1 achievement.
Even one N or P = CL1 NOT ACHIEVED, regardless of other BPs.
